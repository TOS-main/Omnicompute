EdgeMesh

Run one AI model across your own phones and computers, even when they're on different networks. Free and open source.

1. Start your own mesh

One device is the main device: it holds the chat page and hands out the work. A phone works well, since it's always on.

Android phone (recommended)

Install the app. It hosts your mesh in the background, with a notification you can stop it from.

2. Add your other devices

    
On a phone or tablet, just open the permanent contribute link. On a computer, paste one line and enter the code your main device shows. It runs in the background, uses the GPU, and lets you pick how many cores and how much power to use.

Change it later: edgemesh limits --cpu 50 --gpu 80 --cores 4 --awake on. Remove it: edgemesh contribute --uninstall.

  


  

    
Or help the creator's mesh

    
Lend spare power to the public mesh of the person who makes EdgeMesh. You only compute; you never see anyone's chats, and nothing else on your device is shared. Stop any time.
